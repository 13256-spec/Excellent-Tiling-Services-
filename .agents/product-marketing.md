## Product Overview
**One-liner:** A Perth tiling business that sells the tiles, vanities and tapware it installs, with every price online.
**What it does:** Excellent Tiling Services Pty Ltd (17A Olga Road, Maddington WA 6109) sells porcelain tiles,
MELA vanities, basins, tapware, baths, toilets, mirrors and accessories, plus tiling tools and hardware,
online at excellenttilingservice.com.au. Its own licensed team also does bathroom, laundry and tiling jobs
(strip-out, waterproofing, tiling, fit-off) using products from the range. Every job starts with a free
measure and quote (Perth metro).
**Product category:** Tiles and bathroomware store + tiling/bathroom renovation service, Perth.
**Product type:** E-commerce (Shopify, ~430–500 live products) + local trade service + by-appointment showroom.
**Business model:** Retail margin on products (public price = at least 20% profit; MELA priced 5% under the
other stockist, tiles 15% under supplier RRP), trade accounts at 5–25% off per item (tiles max 15%), and paid
installation jobs quoted after a free measure.

**Target customers:**

- Tilers, builders and plumbers (trade accounts, ABN required).
**Decision-makers:** The homeowner (often a couple); for trade, the tiler or builder buying for a job.
**Primary use case:** Getting a bathroom that looks like the photos they saved, without blowing the budget.
**Jobs to be done:**

**Use cases:**
- Full bathroom or laundry renovation (supply and install).
- Quick bathroom refresh: swap the vanity, taps and mirror.

- Trade restocking: hardware and tiles at trade price, picked up in Maddington.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Renovating a bathroom means juggling tile shops, plumbing suppliers and trades, and not knowing the real cost until late.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Big-box stores: wide range but no tiling know-how, and they don't do the install.
- Showrooms: daytime hours only and "call for price".
- Separate tiler + supplier: two parties to coordinate, finger-pointing when something goes wrong.
**What it costs them:** Time off work for showroom visits, budget blowouts, rework from wrong products or quantities.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of an expensive mistake that's tiled in for 20 years; distrust of trades and hidden costs.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Bathroom City — the only other MELA stockist. Some of their MELA prices are lower (62 of 574), so never claim "cheaper than".
**Direct (hardware):** B&B Hardware (Maddington), Terweys Total Hardware (Cannington) — tiling tools and hardware, no installation.
**Direct (tiles):** The tile supplier's own shop and other WA stockists selling at RRP. *Never name the tile supplier publicly.*
**Secondary:** Bunnings-style big-box stores — range and convenience, no specialist advice or install.
**Indirect:** Hiring a builder who supplies everything, or doing nothing (living with the old bathroom).

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- A tiling team that sells what it installs: advice comes from people who lay these tiles.
- Every price is on the website, including trade discounts.
- Evening showroom, Mon–Sat 4:30–9 pm, by appointment, for people who work days.
- Supply and install from one team, with a free measure and quote.
- Free pickup in Maddington; delivery options shown at checkout.
**How we do it differently:** Product range and installation in one business, priced in the open.
**Why that's better:** One point of contact, products the installer trusts, no surprise prices.
**Why customers choose us:** From public reviews: they turn up on time, quote quickly, give good value, do
quality work "with no fuss", and supply the tiles from their own shop. Repeat customers come back for more jobs.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Showroom hours that clash with work, "call for price", juggling a supplier and a tiler separately.
**Pull:** Prices online, evening showroom, one team for products and install, free measure and quote.
**Habit:** "I always go to Bunnings / my builder sorts it."
**Anxiety:** A newer online store they haven't heard of; whether the install will be done well. Answer with: licensed & insured, tiling Perth since 2011 (both on the live Services page), real reviews, and real job photos.

**Business goal:** Grow online sales and installation jobs from free channels (Instagram, Facebook, Marketplace, Perth reno groups, Google Business) without discounting below the 20% profit floor.
**Conversion action:** Buy online; also book a showroom visit, request a free measure and quote, or apply for a trade account.
