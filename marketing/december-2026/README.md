# December 2026 social posts (27 Nov – 26 Dec 2026)

Thirty days of content for Instagram, Facebook and Google Business. Nothing here is scheduled until the owner says yes.
No product repeats anything posted since October (checked against Metricool on 26 Nov 2026); the 22 product posts are
spread across tiles, vanities, basins, tapware, sinks, baths, toilets, mirrors, accessories, shower screens, grout,
silicone, trims and tools. The other eight are the showroom, trade accounts, a tile-quantity tip and the Christmas
closure (closed Fri 18 Dec, back Wed 6 Jan).

## The look (owner, 26 Nov 2026: "not ad-like, not all the same, not corporate")

Photo first on warm paper, a small serif caption and price underneath, no boxes or logo tile. Three background tones
(paper #FBF8F2, stone #E6E0D5, ink #1F2529) and two photo layouts (inset or edge to edge) give variety.

## Where things are

- **Canva (editable, live text):** feed posts `DAHb-Y0niMg` "ETS December 2026 feed posts v2" (1080×1350) and Stories
  `DAHb-QO_V-0` "ETS December 2026 Stories" (1080×1920). 30 pages each, in date order.
- `posts.json`: per page the date, words on the graphic, caption with link and hashtags, `google_text` (the same
  without hashtags, for Google Business), `carousel` (extra clean store photos to follow the designed cover),
  `feed_file`, `story_file`, tone and layout.
- `out/NN.png` feed graphics and `stories/NN.png` Stories, exported from Canva. `img/` the cropped source photos
  (pages 12, 15, 19, 22 and 29 use stock mood photos from `marketing/image-library/`).
- `make_reels.py` builds four weekly Reels into `reels/` from the Story PNGs (slow push-in, dissolves, CC0 music from
  `../finish-it-right/v2/source/music/`).

Copy was fact-checked against the live store on 26 Nov 2026 (`ets-copy-review`, two passes). Prices are as of that day;
re-check before a post goes out after any price change. Tiles tagged `box-units` are quoted per 1.44 m² box with the
per m² figure in the caption. Canva's AI design generator is off for this team, so templates were built by hand.
An earlier navy "price tile" version (Canva `DAHb-EoYTeM`) was rejected by the owner and is not used.
