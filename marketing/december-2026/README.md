# December 2026 social posts (27 Nov – 26 Dec 2026)

One feed post a day at 6:30 pm, Instagram + Facebook, 1080×1350. Thirty posts: products with the live price, two
tile tips, the showroom, trade accounts and the Christmas closure (closed Fri 18 Dec, back Wed 6 Jan).

- **Designs live in Canva**: "ETS December 2026 posts", design `DAHb-EoYTeM`, 30 pages in date order. Every page is the
  same layout (photo, logo tile, headline, navy price tile) with live text, so a word, price or photo can be changed in
  Canva. A sale price uses a blue tile.
- `posts.json`: for each page the date, the words on the graphic, the caption (with product link and hashtags) and the
  Canva photo id. This is the source for scheduling.
- `img/`: the cropped photos used (store product photos; pages 12, 15, 19, 22 and 29 use stock mood photos from
  `marketing/image-library/`). `out/`: the PNGs exported from Canva.

Copy was fact-checked against the live store on 26 Nov 2026 (`ets-copy-review`). Prices are as of that day: re-check
before a post goes out after any price change. MELA vanity prices include the stone top and basin; cut-outs from $35.

Canva's AI design generator is turned off for this team, so the template was built by hand in Canva and duplicated.
