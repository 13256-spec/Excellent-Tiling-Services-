## Facts corrected

1. **Shipping.** 25 old posts (9 main, 16 lunch) said "free shipping in WA on orders over $100" (or "$20 flat rate under $100"). Live Delivery & pickup page and FAQ: free shipping only on orders of **$1,000+**. Under that, small items ship Australia-wide **from $20 by weight**, and basins, mirrors and cabinets go as tracked parcels **from $25**. Tiles and vanities are **Perth metro delivery or free pickup** only.
2. **The $480 FLOVA set** (14 Oct, 28 Oct, 7 Nov) was "delivered free anywhere in WA". It isn't free: at $480 it pays postage by weight.
3. **Vanity "from" price.** Old posts and the S-vanity Story said "from $1,139". The live lowest is **$703** (MELA CLARK 600 with doors). The range is 28 vanities, 600 to 1800 mm, and every one includes a stone top and basin.
4. **Trade account called "free".** The live Trade page doesn't say that, so I dropped the word. Rates are confirmed: 15% off tiles, 5 to 25% off everything else, ABN required, applied automatically when you're logged in.
5. **FLOVA paper holder:** "solid brass" changed to "brass" (that's what the listing says).
6. **Lava Kraka:** the old caption implied 600x1200 comes in matt. On the live site 600x1200 is **gloss only** ($63.70/m²).
7. **CLASSIC 600 and 1200:** handles aren't included (live listing). Added to the copy.
8. **Morgan Hail and Cement Grey:** "from $37.40" is right, but 600x1200 is $50.10/m². Added so the big format isn't mis-sold.
9. **Removed claims we can't prove:** "no tradie needed" and "saves water without feeling weak". Also cut every "every price is online" or "store is open" post (11 Oct, 18 Oct, 3 Nov and the launch hook).
10. **Two calls to action** (book + shop) on the old 13 Oct post were cut to one. Every post now has a single next step.
