# Rollout: discount codes, email automations and free WA post (from 11 Oct 2026)

What went live on Saturday 11 October 2026 and what happens next, week by week. Written for the owner; the
agents (`ets-marketing`, `ets-content`, `ets-copy-review`, `ets-social`, `ets-calendar`, `ets-community`,
`ets-analytics`, `ets-pricing`) pick up the dated jobs. Nothing goes out without `ets-copy-review`.

## Live now (Sat 11 Oct)

| What | Where | Why it makes money |
|---|---|---|
| WELCOME10: 10% off a first order of tools, trims, tapware and bathroom accessories | emailed by the welcome series; the footer sign-up and pop-up promise it and show it after sign-up once the owner publishes the draft theme | a browser becomes an email address, then a first order; margin stays above 22% because the code only works on 20%/25%-band products |
| COMEBACK10: 10% off for customers with no order in 60 days | emailed by the win-back flow the morning after they lapse | a second order from people who already trust us |
| FREEPOST: free standard post on a saved checkout (orders $60+) | emailed 24 h after an abandoned checkout | postage is the usual reason a checkout stalls; costs at most $30 |
| 9 email automations | Klaviyo, see `store-content/email/README.md` | welcome ×3, abandoned checkout ×2, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase ×2 (incl. Google review ask), repeat purchase (day 21), win-back, trade applied, trade approved |
| Free standard post on WA orders of $100 or more (on top of free over $150 Australia-wide) | checkout | matches what the social posts have promised since the Finish It Right campaign |
| Trade pricing on the site: 15% off tiles, 5–25% off the rest | logged-in trade customers | from 11 Oct; application and approval emails are automatic |

## How we'll know it worked

Shopify → Analytics → Sales by discount (WELCOME10 / COMEBACK10 / FREEPOST). Klaviyo → Flows → revenue per
flow. Targets for the first 30 days: the welcome series converts 1 in 10 sign-ups, abandoned-checkout emails
recover 1 in 8 stalled checkouts (the site had 52 checkouts and 7 orders in the 30 days to 11 Oct).
