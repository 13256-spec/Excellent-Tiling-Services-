## Fixed in the theme (draft "ETS 2026 – SEO: lean product schema, no FAQ dupes (draft)", id 145308221503)

A copy of the live theme (145302585407) with two files changed:
- `snippets/ets-product-schema.liquid`: brand, category, specs, dimensions and the description now sit on the
  ProductGroup only; variants inherit them (Google's product-variant guidance). Each offer points at the sitewide
  business by @id for seller and pickup place instead of repeating the logo and address. Maxisil Wet Area Silicone's
  JSON-LD drops from 145 KB to under 100 KB; the 220-variant BAT tiling angle from 887 KB to about 500 KB. Nothing
  Google reads for merchant listings is lost (price, availability, shipping, returns, sku, gtin, image per variant).
- `sections/ets-faq.liquid`: the FAQPage schema is no longer printed on product pages. The same five questions were on
  all 1,339 product pages; Google only wants FAQPage markup for questions specific to that page, and since 2023 shows
  FAQ rich results only for government and health sites, so the markup there was risk without reward. The visible
  FAQ block is unchanged, and the home, trade and tiling-services pages keep their FAQ schema.
Checked in the preview: product pages render, every JSON-LD block parses, no FAQPage on products.
