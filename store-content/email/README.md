Who gets them: abandoned checkout, abandoned cart, post-purchase and win-back go to anyone Klaviyo can email who
has not unsubscribed (Klaviyo's "can receive email marketing"); the welcome series only to subscribed profiles.
The two trade emails go to everyone who joins the segment (the account is not enabled for Klaviyo
"transactional" emails, so they are ordinary emails with an unsubscribe link). Smart sending (skip anyone
emailed in the last 16 hours) is on for everything except the first welcome email and the two trade emails.

Segment-triggered flows only fire for people who join the segment after the flow went live, so nobody who
was already a subscriber, trade customer or lapsed customer on 11 Oct gets a surprise email.

## The emails

Masters live in Klaviyo under Content → Templates, named `ETS | …`. Sources are in `templates/` here
(`<key>.html`, `.txt`, `.subject`), generated by `make_emails.py` (`python3 make_emails.py <output dir>`).
Layout: logo header, navy headings, one blue button, plain Aussie copy, footer with address, phone, showroom
hours and Klaviyo's unsubscribe link. Dynamic bits use Klaviyo's Shopify event data: `event.extra.line_items`
(product title, first image, variant, quantity, line price) and `event.extra.checkout_url`.

**Editing an email.** Each flow holds its own copy of the template (Klaviyo copies it when the flow is
created), so changing the master does not change the live flow. Edit the email inside the flow (Flows → open
the flow → click the email → Edit content), or change the master and rebuild the flow. Keep the
`{% unsubscribe 'Unsubscribe' %}` tag in the HTML (Klaviyo turns it into the unsubscribe link; don't wrap it
in another link) and `{% unsubscribe_link %}` in the plain-text version.

## Discount codes used

WELCOME10, COMEBACK10 and FREEPOST: see `store-content/pricing/PRICING-PLAYBOOK.md`, "Discount codes". Both
10% codes only work on products in the 20% and 25% trade bands, so profit stays above 22% of the price.

## How to check it is working

- Klaviyo → Flows: each flow shows recipients, opens, clicks and revenue.
- Quick test: subscribe on the site (footer sign-up or pop-up) with an address Klaviyo doesn't already know;
  the first welcome email arrives within a few minutes (and, once the draft theme is published, the pop-up shows
  WELCOME10 straight after sign-up).
- Weekly: ask the `ets-analytics` agent for the flow numbers.
