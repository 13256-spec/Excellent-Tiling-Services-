# New look for the website (12 Oct 2026)

The owner asked, after the postage fix, to "redo how the site looks to attract more customers". This is the audit of
the site as it stood (the postage draft, published by the owner the same evening), the direction chosen, what was built,
and what is left for the owner. Everything was built in a copy of the live theme; nothing changes for customers until the
owner publishes the copy ("ETS 2026 – New look (draft 12 Oct)", preview link in the message to the owner and at the end
of this file).

One honest note first: looks turn visitors into buyers, they do not bring visitors. The 30-day numbers in
`sales-plan-2026-10-12.md` show the site converting at about 2.5% of real visits, which is normal for a shop like this; the
shortage is visitors (about 320 real ones a month). The Google Ads fix, Search Console and reviews in the owner's to-do
list are what bring people. The new look makes the most of the people who arrive.

## What the audit found (phone and desktop screenshots, before images in `img/`)

Good bones: a custom theme with a real structure (hero, departments, product rails, tile ranges, renovations, trade,
delivery, FAQ), navy and stone colours that match the logo, decent product photography for the big tile ranges and MELA.

What held it back:

1. **The first products a visitor saw were the worst photos.** The Tiles collection was sorted by "best selling", which with
   no sales history meant newest first: eight mosaic tiles with 175-pixel images, blurry on every screen. They led the
   homepage "Shop the range" rail and the Tiles page, and the Tiles collection banner was the first of them, blown up.
2. **Three looks fighting:** a serif that never loaded (Instrument Serif was named but not served, so headings fell back to
   the system Georgia/Palatino on product pages), a geometric sans elsewhere, pill buttons, double-bezel rounded cards with
   soft shadows, a brass accent, and gold edges on the chat widget and cookie banner. It read as a software product, not a
   tile showroom.
3. **Department tiles** mixed a lifestyle photo, product cut-outs on beige with a multiply blend, and a line drawing, each
   with a navy arrow bubble; different sizes made a bento grid that never ended square on laptops.
4. **Hero on phones:** eyebrow text overlapped the "Pictured" caption; four calls to action (two buttons, two text links)
   plus a stats row; copy over a dark gradient on a small screen.
5. **Product page:** "Taxes included. Shipping calculated at checkout" under the price contradicted the postage line we
   added; the trade notice sat between the price and the options; the colour options of a product like Maxisil (26 colours)
   were full-width buttons, so on a phone the Add to cart button was nine screens down.
6. **Cart page on desktop:** the free-post bar I added on 12 Oct landed inside the order summary's grid and overlapped
   "Estimated total" (a bug in that morning's work, fixed in the live theme before the owner published).
7. **Collection pages** showed the breadcrumb twice (in the banner and again above the filters).

## Direction: porcelain and grout

The showroom sells surfaces, so the site should look like one: white and porcelain surfaces, thin "grout" lines instead of
shadows and rounded shells, square corners, one typeface and one accent colour.

| Token | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Paper (page) | `#F3F2EE` | a touch cooler than before, closer to porcelain than to cream |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | product photos come on white; cards are white with a line, not a shadow |
| Stone | `#E8E6E0` | secondary surfaces (trade note, inputs) |
| Grout line | `#D9D6CF` / strong `#B9B5AC` | the one structural device: 1 px lines between tiles, columns, list rows |
| Ink | `#15213A` | the logo's navy; text, top bar, trade band, footer |
| Accent | `#2A5DA8` (hover `#214B8A`), light tint `#E4EEF9`, sky `#58A0D8`/`#9CC8EE` on dark | taken from the logo's blue diamond; used on everything you can buy or book (Add to cart, Checkout, Shop tiles, Book a quote) and nothing else |
| Trade green | `#1F6149` | unchanged, functional |
| Type | Archivo (Google Fonts, variable, 400–800) | one grotesque for display and body; 600 weight, tight tracking for headings; tabular figures on prices. Manrope, Instrument Serif and the brass accent are gone |
| Corners | 4 px controls, 6 px panels, 2 px badges | square like a tile edge; pills removed everywhere (buttons, chips, search, inputs, variant buttons, badges) |

The memorable element is the department wall on the homepage: ten equal square tiles, five across (two on phones),
separated by 1 px grout, each a product on white with its name under a line. No arrows, no shadows, no sizes.

## What was built (theme copy "ETS 2026 – New look (draft 12 Oct)")

- **Tokens and type** (`assets/ets-2026.css`, `layout/theme.liquid`, new `assets/ets-newlook.css` loaded last): the table
  above, applied to the header, search, navigation, menu drawer, buttons, links, cards, rails, tabs, trust strip,
  renovations split, trade band, delivery columns, FAQ, footer, newsletter, pop-up, forms, product page extras, chat widget
  and cookie banner. The old brass variable now points at the blue, so every rule that used it followed.
- **Hero** (`sections/ets-hero-full.liquid`): headline, one line, two buttons (blue, white outline), the "Pictured" tag;
  eyebrow, quick links and the stats row removed. On phones the photo sits on top (4:3) and the words sit on paper under it.
- **Department wall** (`sections/ets-categories.liquid`, `templates/index.json`): all ten tiles the same size; Tiles and
  Mirrors keep a room photo, the rest show the product cut-out on white.
- **Shop the range / Tiles collection** (live, not theme): the Tiles collection is now sorted by hand, big-format ranges
  first (Morgan, Time Stone, Cement, Lava, Particle, Terrazzo, then the other porcelain, then ceramic wall tiles, mosaics
  last), and its banner image is the Time Stone living room. This also fixed the homepage rail.
- **Product page** (`templates/product.json`, `templates/product.store-wide.json`, `config/settings_data.json`,
  `snippets/ets-pdp-perks.liquid`): price, options, Add to cart, postage line, then the trade note; Shopify's tax line off
  and "Prices include GST" added to the postage line; option buttons hug their text (26 colours now fit in nine short rows
  instead of 26 full-width ones); buy buttons blue; sticky Add to cart stays on.
- **Cart** (`sections/main-cart.liquid`, `snippets/ets-shipping-bar.liquid`): the free-post bar sits under the items on
  every screen size; blue fill. (The placement fix is also in the live theme.)
- **Collection pages**: second breadcrumb hidden; chips and cards squared.
- Previewed with Playwright at 1440 and 390 px on the home, Tiles, Maxisil, Morgan Storm and cart pages, in the
  Australian market context; after images in `img/`.

## For the owner

1. Open the preview link on your phone and on a computer. If you like it: Online Store → Themes → "ETS 2026 – New look
   (draft 12 Oct)" → ⋯ → Publish. If you want something changed, say what and it changes in the draft first.
2. Keep one backup theme. All the earlier copies were deleted when the postage theme was published, so the only copy of the
   old look is in this repo (`theme/`), which is enough to rebuild but slower than a backup in Shopify. "Duplicate" on the
   live theme before publishing anything is a good habit.
3. Reviews and photos still matter more than any colour: install Judge.me (in the to-do list) and send a phone photo of
   the 20 products without one.
