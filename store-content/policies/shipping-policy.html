This policy explains how Excellent Tiling Services Pty Ltd (ABN 63 150 082 054) ("ETS", "we", "us") dispatches and delivers orders placed through excellenttilingservice.com.au.

Where we deliver

We deliver within Australia only. We do not ship internationally. Our fulfilment location is in Maddington, Western Australia.

Product availability and dispatch

Some products are held in stock by ETS. Others need to be ordered in from, or collected from, our suppliers before they can be dispatched. Because of this, product availability and dispatch timing can vary from product to product and from order to order.

If your order includes a mix of products, some items may be ready before others. If there is a delay with any part of your order, we will aim to let you know as soon as we can.

How each type of product is delivered

How your order is delivered, and what it costs, depends on the products in it. The options and charges for your order and address are always shown at checkout before you pay.

Small bathroom items: Australia-wide, free over $100

Tapware (basin, wall and sink mixers, spouts), shower heads, arms and shower sets, towel rails, hooks, toilet roll holders, soap and shelf holders, toilet brush holders and similar small boxed items ship anywhere in Australia:

  • free standard shipping when these items total $100 or more; or
  • a $20 flat rate when they total less than $100.

Bulky parcels: Australia Post, priced by weight

Basins, mirrors and mirror cabinets, kitchen sinks, freestanding bath spouts and mixers, niche boxes, floor wastes and some hardware items are sent by Australia Post tracked parcel. The charge is based on the total weight of these items and is shown at checkout. Orders over 44 kg may need a freight quote.

Large items and tiles: Perth metro delivery or pickup

Tiles, trims, baths, toilets, vanities, tallboys, shower screens and other large, heavy or fragile items are delivered locally within the Perth metro area, or can be collected from Maddington. Where local delivery is available for your address, it is offered at checkout. Tiles are not shipped outside the Perth metro area. For other large items going outside Perth, please contact us before ordering and we will let you know your options and quote freight.

If no suitable delivery option is shown for your order, please contact us before placing it. For some orders we may contact you directly to confirm delivery details or arrangements, for example where an order includes large, heavy or fragile items, or where the delivery address is in a regional or remote area.

Delivery timeframes

Any dispatch or delivery timeframe we provide, whether at checkout, by email, by phone or on our website, is an estimate only. Actual timing can depend on supplier availability, freight carrier schedules, your location, weather and other factors outside our control.

Free local pickup (Maddington)

You can choose free local pickup at checkout and collect your order from 17A Olga Road, Maddington WA 6109. Our premises are open by appointment only, Monday to Saturday from 4:30 pm to 9 pm (closed Sunday), so please:

  • wait until we have confirmed your order is ready for pickup;
  • book a pickup time through our booking page or by calling 0450 960 543; and
  • bring your order number with you.

For large or heavy items, please bring a suitable vehicle and let us know when you book if you need help loading.

Large, heavy and fragile items

Tiles, vanities, baths, mirrors, glass, stone and other large, heavy or fragile products may need specialised delivery arrangements. To help your delivery go smoothly and safely:

  • provide a complete and accurate delivery address, and a phone number we or the carrier can reach you on;
  • tell us at the time of ordering about anything that may affect access, such as stairs, narrow or steep driveways, limited truck access, building site conditions or restricted delivery hours;
  • make sure there is a safe, clear and accessible place for the goods to be unloaded; and
  • arrange for someone to be available to receive the delivery where required, and to help move heavy items once they have been unloaded.

Drivers may not be able to unload goods where it is unsafe to do so, or where the site cannot be safely accessed. If a delivery cannot be completed for these reasons, we will contact you to discuss alternative arrangements. If those arrangements involve any extra cost, we will discuss this with you before proceeding.

Checking your delivery

Please check your order as soon as possible after it arrives. If anything is damaged, missing or incorrect, please contact us promptly with your order number and clear photos of the products and packaging. Where possible, please note any visible damage on the delivery paperwork. See our Refund Policy for more information.
