Excellent Tiling Services Pty Ltd (ABN 63 150 082 054) ("ETS", "we", "us") runs the online store at excellenttilingservices.com, our showroom in Maddington, Western Australia, and a tiling and renovation business in Perth (together, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect your personal information, and how you can access or correct it or make a complaint.

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. Our online store is powered by Shopify. If there is a conflict between our Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, this Privacy Policy applies to how we handle your personal information.

What personal information we collect

"Personal information" means information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable. Depending on how you deal with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details, such as your name, phone number, email address, and billing and delivery addresses.
  • Order and transaction details, such as the products you view, add to your cart, buy, return or exchange, and your delivery and pickup choices.
  • Payment information. Card payments are processed by Shopify and its payment providers. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Account details, such as your login email, preferences, order history and, for trade accounts, your business name, ABN and trade type.
  • Booking and quote details you give us when you book a showroom visit or pickup time, or request a quote or measure, such as your suburb or site address, the type and size of the job, your preferred dates and times, and any photos or plans you send us.
  • Communications, including emails, phone calls, messages and form submissions, and any feedback or reviews you give us.
  • Device and usage information, such as your IP address, browser and device type, and how you use our website, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Marketing preferences, such as whether you have subscribed to our emails.

We collect most personal information directly from you. We may also collect it from Shopify and our other service providers, from delivery carriers (for example, delivery status), from someone acting for you (such as your builder), or from public sources such as the ABN Lookup when we check a trade account application.

You can browse our website without telling us who you are. If you do not give us the information we ask for, we may not be able to process your order, provide a quote, approve a trade account or respond to your enquiry.

How we use your personal information

We use personal information to:

  • process and deliver your orders, arrange pickups, and handle returns, refunds and warranty claims;
  • manage your customer or trade account, including checking trade account applications and showing trade pricing;
  • arrange showroom appointments, measures and quotes, and carry out tiling or renovation work you engage us for;
  • answer your questions and provide customer support;
  • send you marketing emails, if you have agreed to receive them;
  • run, secure and improve our website, including through analytics, and prevent fraud and misuse; and
  • comply with our legal obligations, such as tax and record-keeping requirements, and resolve disputes.

Marketing

We only send marketing emails if you have subscribed or otherwise agreed to receive them. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to stop at any time. We will still send you messages about your orders, bookings and account.

We may use Shopify and advertising platforms such as Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) to show you ads for our products, including ads based on your activity on our website. You can manage this through your browser's cookie settings, the cookie banner on our website where shown, and the ad settings of those platforms.

Who we disclose personal information to

We do not sell your personal information. We disclose it only as needed for the purposes above, to:

  • Shopify, which hosts our store and processes orders and payments for us;
  • payment processors, for payments and refunds;
  • delivery partners such as Australia Post and local couriers, so they can deliver your order;
  • suppliers and manufacturers, where needed to fill your order or assess a warranty claim;
  • providers of the tools we use to run the business, such as email, forms, booking, analytics and IT services;
  • our professional advisers, such as our accountant, lawyers and insurers; and
  • government agencies or others where the law requires or allows it.

Our service providers may only use your personal information to provide services to us.

Relationship with Shopify

Our store is hosted by Shopify, which collects and processes personal information about your access to and use of the store in order to provide and improve its services. Information you submit through the store is transmitted to and shared with Shopify and its service providers, which may be located outside Australia. We also use certain Shopify features that draw on your interactions with our store, other merchants and Shopify. For those features, Shopify is responsible for how it processes your personal information, including responding to your requests about it. To learn more, see the Shopify Consumer Privacy Policy. You may be able to exercise rights over that information through the Shopify Privacy Portal.

Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Australia, including in Canada, the United States, the European Union and other countries where Shopify, Google, Meta and our other providers operate. When we use overseas providers, we take reasonable steps to make sure they handle your personal information in a way that is consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep your cart working, remember your preferences, understand how the site is used and support advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the store, such as the cart and checkout, may not work properly without them.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, change or disclosure. These include using reputable hosted platforms, limiting who can access customer information, and using secure, password-protected accounts. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so please do not send us sensitive information such as card numbers by email.

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, or as long as the law requires. For example, we must keep tax and transaction records for at least five years. When we no longer need it, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask us for access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading. You can also ask us to delete it, and we will do so unless we need to keep it, for example to meet a legal obligation. Contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will respond within a reasonable time, usually within 30 days. If we refuse a request, we will tell you why and how to complain.

Children

Our Services are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Complaints

If you have a concern or complaint about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first using the details below. We will look into it and respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will publish the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
